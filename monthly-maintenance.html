Services/Monthly Maintenance

Fix it before it fails.

Your equipment runs great today — and it can fail any time. On a monthly schedule, a technician inspects your equipment to make sure it's working and performing at its best.

Why monthly

Clients on maintenance spend less on repairs.

Monthly service maintenance reduces equipment failure and catches issues before they become worse — and more expensive. It's the difference between a scheduled 20-minute adjustment and a lost clinic day.

01
Scheduled, not reactive

A technician visits on a monthly schedule, so inspection happens around your calendar — not in the middle of it.

02
Every unit inspected

Vacuum, compressor, chairs, delivery units, sterilizers and small equipment — checked to make sure each is performing at its best.

03
Small fixes, early

Worn parts and small issues are addressed on the spot, before they turn into a failure and an emergency call.

04
Longer equipment life

Regular service prolongs the life of your tools and protects the investment in your operatory.

Between visits

Equipment care your team can do daily.

A few minutes of care between visits keeps equipment reliable and makes every monthly inspection faster.

After every patient
Flush handpieces and syringes

Flush water through handpieces and air/water syringes after every patient to keep lines clear.

Between patients
Lubricate and sterilize

Lubricate and sterilize handpieces; lubricate prophy angles and contra-angles between patients.

Daily
Keep suction moving

Run suction cleaner through the HVE and saliva ejector. Clean suction traps weekly.

Daily
Drain the ultrasonic cleaner

Drain the ultrasonic cleaner daily to protect the tank and keep cleaning cycles effective.

Each evening
Empty water bottles

Empty the water bottles on each delivery unit at the end of the day.

Weekend
Sterilizer cleaning cycle

Run a sterilizer cleaning cycle and empty units over the weekend so Monday starts clean.
